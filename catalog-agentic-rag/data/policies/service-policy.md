# Service Policy

**Applies to:** CircuitNova and Hearthwell
**Owner:** Group Service Operations
**Version:** 1.4, effective 2026-03-01

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the service plans and where service may be sold.

## 2. Serviceable and non-serviceable items

2.1 In `where_we_sell`, `serviceability = Serviceable` means a service plan can be sold with the item.

2.2 Every serviceable item must have a `service_plan` and a list of `serviceable_countries`.

2.3 Non-serviceable items must not have a service plan.

## 3. Service plans

Plans are numbered `SVC-01` to `SVC-18` and fall into three tiers:

| Plans | Tier | What's included | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| SVC-01 to SVC-06 | **Basic** | Replacement parts only, customer ships the item in | 1 year |
| SVC-07 to SVC-12 | **Standard** | Parts and labour, on-site visit within 5 working days | 2 years |
| SVC-13 to SVC-18 | **Premium** | Parts and labour, on-site within 24 hours, 24/7 phone support | 3 years |

## 4. Where service may be sold

4.1 A service plan may only be sold in the countries listed in `serviceable_countries`.

4.2 If a serviceable item is **quotable in a country where no service is offered**, sales must quote it
**without** a service plan in that country and tell the customer.

4.3 Note: `serviceable_countries` and `quotable_countries` both list countries separated by `;`
(`AT;BE;DE`).

## 5. Expired or obsolete items

5.1 No new service plans may be sold for items that are expired (see the Product Lifecycle Policy).
